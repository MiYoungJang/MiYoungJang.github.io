I am a (tenure-track) professor at Dongduk Women's University.

My research is in Machine Learning for scientific discovery and industry. I obtained my PhD in Mathematics from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, advised by Sheldon Katz.

Before joining Dongduk Women's University, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Pennsylvania (2017–2020). I then joined Samsung Electronics, where I worked at the Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology (2021–2024) and later at the AI Center (2024–2026), first as a Staff Researcher and then as a Principal Researcher.
